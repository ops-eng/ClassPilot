**ClassPilot - School PLanner** is a focused planner for students and teachers that brings courses, lessons, assignments, exams and study tasks into one clean, reliable app. 

Built for clarity and speed, **ClassPilot** helps you plan a semester, manage daily lessons, track grades, and stay on top of deadlines — whether you’re trying the app for free or running a full classroom workflow on Pro.

### Core capabilities:

	•	Course management: create and organize courses with metadata (term, instructor, color, notes).
	•	Lesson planner: schedule lessons with start/end times, locations, and quick links to course materials.
	•	Assignments & exams: add assignments and exams with due dates, priorities, estimated effort, and status.
	•	Tasks & checklists: lightweight tasks with subtasks, priorities, and completion history.
	•	Grades: full grade visibility and gradebook per course with weighted categories and quick grade entry.
	•	Study cards: create flashcards for quick review and spaced practice.
	•	Documents & attachments: attach documents; preview and open files from the app.
	•	Reminders & notifications: set reminders for items and receive local notifications.
	•	Appearance & themes: choose light/dark and theme accents.
	•	Import options: import course data from standard LMS exports.
	•	Local backups: export and restore data.
	•	Dashboard: concise dashboard showing next lesson, upcoming deadlines, and quick actions.
	•	Security & privacy: iCloud sync for cross‑device continuity.
	•	Deep links & sharing: open specific lessons, assignments or courses from links and share summaries with classmates or teachers.
	•	Performance: timeline views optimized for smooth scrolling and clear event placement (day and week planners).

### Extra Advanced Pro features:

	•	Home‑screen widgets: full home widgets (Next Lesson, Today, Assignments, Exams, Course) with multi‑instance support and per‑widget configuration.
	•	iCloud sync & background sync: automatic cross‑device sync and conflict resolution.
	•	Expanded imports: direct imports from MSTeams, Moodle, Google Classroom, Canvas.
	•	Dashboard customization: add, remove and rearrange dashboard modules and widgets.
	•	Calendar sync: two‑way calendar integration with system calendars and advanced sync options.
	•	Advanced exports & sharing: export full course packages, share read/write links, and bulk CSV/ICS export.
	•	Priority support & migration help: faster support and guided migration for schools and power users.

### Usability & reliability:
	•	Deterministic day and week timeline rendering for clear, non‑overlapping event placement.
	•	Overlap resolution and columned event layout for readable schedules.
	•	Batched updates and efficient rendering to avoid visual artifacts and ghosting in timeline views.
	•	Test cases and diagnostics built into the app to reproduce and fix layout issues quickly.
